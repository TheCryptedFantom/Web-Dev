Dinosaurs

Dinosaurs with a mix of strengths and weaknesses.

  • T-Rex: Powerful jaws, but small arms
  • Brontosaurus: Huge size, but slow
  • Stegosaurus: Armored back, but slow
  • Velociraptor: Fast and agile, but small
  • Triceratops: Three horns for defense, but slow
  • Parasaurolophus: Crest for communication, but vulnerable
  • Spinosaurus: Sail on back, but semi-aquatic
  • Allosaurus: Large Predator/Carnivore, powerful bite
  • Carnotaurus: Horned head, fast runner
  • Giganotosaurus: Even larger than T-Rex, powerful bite
