Sensei Wu

Master and Mentor of the Ninja

Sensei Wu is an ancient master of Spinjitzu and the mentor of the Ninja. He has spent much of his life protecting Ninjago and preparing the next generation of heroes.

Master of Spinjitzu

Wu is an incredibly skilled martial artist and master of Spinjitzu. He has spent centuries studying combat, history, and the ancient secrets of Ninjago.

Family

Sensei Wu is the son of the First Spinjitzu Master and the brother of Lord Garmadon. This makes him the uncle of Lloyd.

The Ninja

Wu brought the original Ninja together and trained them in the ways of Spinjitzu. Over the years, he became more than their teacher. He became a mentor and trusted member of their family.

Wu and Garmadon

Wu and Garmadon have a complicated relationship. Despite being brothers, their lives have taken very different paths. Wu has repeatedly tried to help his brother while also protecting Ninjago from his actions.

Weapons

Wu is most famously associated with his staff. He has also demonstrated incredible skill with many different weapons throughout his long life.

Wu's Wisdom

One of Wu's most important roles is teaching the Ninja lessons that go beyond fighting. He encourages them to learn from their mistakes, work together, and understand the responsibility that comes with their abilities.

Wu's History

Wu's history stretches back to the earliest days of Ninjago. His connection to the First Spinjitzu Master, Garmadon, and the ancient history of the world makes him one of the most knowledgeable characters in the series.

Fun Facts

  • Wu is the Ninja's teacher and mentor.
  • He is the brother of Garmadon.
  • He is Lloyd's uncle.
  • Wu is the son of the First Spinjitzu Master.
  • He is a master of Spinjitzu.
