Welcome to Ninjago

Welcome to the world of Ninjago, a world filled with legendary warriors, powerful elements, dragons, ancient secrets, and dangerous villains.

Follow the Ninja as they protect Ninjago and the people they care about. Along the way, they face powerful enemies, discover more about themselves, and learn what it truly means to be a hero.

The Ninja

The Ninja are a team of warriors trained by Sensei Wu. Each member has their own elemental power, personality, abilities, and history.

Kai

Master of Fire and one of the original Ninja.

Zane

Master of Ice and a Nindroid with a strong sense of compassion.

Jay

Master of Lightning, inventor, and the team's resident comedian.

Cole

Master of Earth and one of the team's strongest warriors.

Lloyd

The legendary Green Ninja and master of Energy.

Nia

Master of Water and a skilled warrior and engineer.

Sensei Wu

Sensei Wu is the Ninja's teacher and mentor. He is an ancient master of Spinjitzu who has spent much of his life protecting Ninjago and preparing the Ninja for the challenges ahead.

The World of Ninjago

Ninjago is much larger than the city where the Ninja live. Throughout their adventures, the Ninja visit different realms, ancient kingdoms, mysterious islands, and dangerous dimensions.

Some important locations and realms include Ninjago City, the Monastery of Spinjitzu, the First Realm, the Never-Realm, and the Underworld.

The Story

The story of Ninjago spans many years and follows the Ninja through countless battles and adventures. Their enemies range from ancient serpentine warriors to powerful elemental masters, ghosts, pirates, Oni, and other supernatural threats.

As the Ninja grow older, their responsibilities grow with them. They must learn to work together while also dealing with their own personal challenges.
