Zane

Master of Ice

Zane is the elemental master of Ice and one of the most intelligent members of the Ninja. He is calm, thoughtful, and always willing to put others before himself.

Zane is different from the other Ninja because he is a Nindroid, an artificial being created by Dr. Julien.

Element: Ice

Zane can create and control ice. He can freeze objects, create ice-based attacks, and use his elemental abilities in combat.

The Nindroid

Zane eventually discovers that he is not a human but a Nindroid created by Dr. Julien. Although he was built artificially, Zane develops emotions, friendships, and his own sense of right and wrong.

Personality

  • Intelligent
  • Calm
  • Logical
  • Selfless
  • Compassionate
  • Loyal

Family and Friends

Zane was created by Dr. Julien, who became like a father to him. Zane also develops a close relationship with Pixal, another Nindroid who becomes an important part of his life.

Zane's Story

Zane's story explores what it means to be human. Throughout his adventures, he repeatedly demonstrates that being human is about more than simply being biologically human.

His willingness to sacrifice himself for his friends becomes one of the defining parts of his character.

Fun Facts

  • Zane is a Nindroid.
  • His elemental power is Ice.
  • Dr. Julien created Zane.
  • Zane is known for his intelligence and selflessness.
