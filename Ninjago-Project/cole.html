Cole

Master of Earth

Cole is the elemental master of Earth and one of the strongest members of the Ninja. He is dependable, hardworking, and often acts as the team's foundation.

Element: Earth

Cole can control Earth and use its power to create powerful attacks. His elemental abilities complement his already impressive physical strength.

Personality

  • Strong
  • Dependable
  • Responsible
  • Kind
  • Protective
  • Hardworking

Family

Cole's father is Lou, a famous musician. His mother, Lilly, was the previous elemental master of Earth.

Weapons

Cole is famously associated with a large scythe. He has also used many other weapons throughout his adventures.

Cole's Story

Cole has faced many challenges throughout his time as a Ninja. His adventures have tested his strength, leadership, and willingness to protect others.

Even when the team is facing overwhelming odds, Cole consistently tries to keep everyone together.

Fun Facts

  • Cole is the elemental master of Earth.
  • His mother Lilly was the previous Master of Earth.
  • Cole is known for his physical strength.
  • His father Lou is a musician.
