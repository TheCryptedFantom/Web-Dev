Jay

Master of Lightning

Jay is the elemental master of Lightning and one of the most energetic members of the Ninja. He is an inventor, fighter, and comedian who often uses humor during difficult situations.

Element: Lightning

Jay can generate and control electricity and lightning. His elemental abilities make him a powerful fighter and allow him to use electrical attacks against his enemies.

Personality

  • Funny
  • Energetic
  • Creative
  • Clever
  • Nervous
  • Loyal

Family

Jay was raised by his adoptive parents, Ed and Edna Walker. Although they are not his biological parents, Jay considers them his family.

Jay the Inventor

Jay is an excellent inventor and engineer. He frequently creates machines, vehicles, and other inventions that help the Ninja during their missions.

Jay's Story

Jay begins his journey as a somewhat nervous and inexperienced member of the Ninja. Over time, he becomes more confident in himself and learns how important he is to the team.

His relationships with his teammates, especially Nya, become an important part of his story.

Fun Facts

  • Jay is the elemental master of Lightning.
  • He is an inventor and engineer.
  • Ed and Edna are his adoptive parents.
  • Jay is known for making jokes during stressful situations.
