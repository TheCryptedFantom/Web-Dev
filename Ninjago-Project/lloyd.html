Lloyd

The Green Ninja

Lloyd is the legendary Green Ninja and one of the central heroes of Ninjago. He begins his journey as a young and inexperienced member of the team before eventually becoming one of its most important leaders.

Element: Life

Lloyd possesses the elemental power of Life. His abilities have developed significantly throughout his adventures, making him one of the most powerful Ninja.

The Green Ninja

Lloyd becomes the Green Ninja after a prophecy reveals that he will become one of Ninjago's greatest heroes. His journey to becoming the Green Ninja is one of the most important stories in the series.

Family

Lloyd is the son of Lord Garmadon and Misako. He is also the nephew of Sensei Wu and the grandson of the First Spinjitzu Master.

Personality

  • Brave
  • Determined
  • Compassionate
  • Responsible
  • Protective
  • Sometimes burdened by leadership

Lloyd as a Leader

As Lloyd grows older, he takes on more responsibility for the Ninja. Being a leader often means making difficult decisions and putting the needs of others before himself.

Lloyd's Story

Lloyd's story is largely about growing from a young boy into a powerful warrior and leader. His journey involves discovering his abilities, understanding his family, and learning how to carry the responsibility of being the Green Ninja.

Fun Facts

  • Lloyd is the Green Ninja.
  • His elemental power was always thought to be Energy early on, but newer episodes show it is Life.
  • He is the son of Garmadon.
  • Sensei Wu is his uncle.
  • Lloyd becomes one of the team's main leaders.
