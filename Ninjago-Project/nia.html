Nia

Master of Water

Nia is the elemental master of Water and one of the most skilled warriors in the Ninja team. She is intelligent, determined, independent, and never afraid to stand up for what she believes is right.

Element: Water

Nia can control and manipulate water. Her elemental abilities allow her to create powerful attacks and use water in a variety of ways.

Samurai X

Before becoming the Master of Water, Nia secretly operated as Samurai X. Using her engineering skills, she created armor and equipment that allowed her to fight alongside the Ninja.

Personality

  • Independent
  • Intelligent
  • Determined
  • Courageous
  • Protective
  • Confident

Family

Nia is Kai's younger sister. Their parents are Ray and Maya, both of whom have connections to the elemental powers of Ninjago.

Nia's Story

Nia's journey begins with her secretly becoming Samurai X. She eventually discovers her own elemental power and becomes the Master of Water.

Her story focuses heavily on independence, identity, and learning to accept the responsibility that comes with her powers.

Fun Facts

  • Nia is the elemental master of Water.
  • She is Kai's sister.
  • She was originally known as Samurai X.
  • Nia is an excellent engineer.
